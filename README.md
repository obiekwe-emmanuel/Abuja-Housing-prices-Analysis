# Abuja Housing Prices Analysis

An Excel-based analysis of residential property asking prices and listing patterns across 16 areas of the Federal Capital Territory (FCT), Abuja, Nigeria.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes residential property listings for rent and sale using an interactive Excel workbook. It contains a landing page and four dashboards examining rental prices, sale prices, listing counts, property types, bedroom categories, and price ranges.

### Project Details

- **Project Name:** Abuja Housing Prices Analysis
- **Workbook:** `Abuja Housing Prices Analysis.xlsm`
- **Primary Tool:** Microsoft Excel
- **Dataset:** Abuja Housing Prices v1
- **Data Source:** Groundwork Data on Kaggle, using listings scraped from PropertyPro.ng in April 2026
- **Geographic Coverage:** 16 areas of Abuja's Federal Capital Territory
- **Total Records:** 481 listings
- **Rental Listings:** 250
- **Sale Listings:** 231

## Project Objectives

- Analyze median advertised rental and sale prices across Abuja's 16 areas.
- Examine listing distributions across different price ranges.
- Identify the most represented property types and bedroom categories.
- Compare listing concentrations across locations.
- Present findings through interactive Excel dashboards to support property-market research and further investigation.

## Key Findings

### Rental Market

- **Median Annual Rent:** ₦7.5 million.
- **Advertised Rent Range:** ₦800,000 to ₦250 million.
- **Property Type:** Flats/apartments account for 110 of 250 rental listings, representing **44%** of the rental sample.
- **Location:** Guzape has the highest rental listing count, with 21 listings, while Asokoro has 4.

### Property Sales Market

- **Median Sale Asking Price:** ₦260 million.
- **Advertised Sale Price Range:** ₦36.5 million to ₦5 billion.
- **Price Bands:** The ₦250 million to below ₦750 million category contains 93 listings (**40.3%**), while the ₦50 million to below ₦250 million category contains 92 listings (**39.8%**). Together, they represent **80.1%** of sale listings.
- **Property Type:** Detached duplexes account for 76 of 231 sale listings (**32.9%**), followed by flats/apartments with 63 listings (**27.3%**).
- **Bedroom Category:** Four-bedroom properties are the most represented sale category, with 79 listings (**34.2%**).
- **Location:** Lokogoma has the highest sale listing count in the sample, with 20 listings.

These findings describe the listings included in the dataset and should not be interpreted as a complete representation of Abuja's housing market.

## Methodology

The analysis uses residential property listing data covering rental and sale properties across 16 areas of Abuja.

The dashboards examine:

- Median advertised prices by location.
- Median prices by bedroom count.
- Median prices by property type.
- Listing counts by area and property type.
- Listing distribution across price bands.
- Summary statistics, including total listings, median prices, minimum prices, and maximum prices.

Median prices are used to describe typical asking-price levels while reducing the influence of unusually high or low values. Price bands group listings into defined ranges for comparison.

## Limitations

- The dataset represents listings collected from PropertyPro.ng in April 2026 and is not a census of all residential properties in Abuja.
- Advertised asking prices may differ from negotiated or completed transaction prices.
- Listing counts indicate the number of records in the dataset, not necessarily housing demand, sales volume, rental absorption, or market liquidity.
- Some locations and property categories may have relatively small sample sizes.
- Differences in property condition, floor area, facilities, and other characteristics may influence prices but are not necessarily fully captured in the dataset.
- The analysis is descriptive and does not establish causal relationships.
